01 About

I'm a computer vision and deep learning researcher with more than three years of experience building production computer vision systems — from object detection on satellite imagery to medical imaging. I'm currently a Research Assistant at DFKI and Universität des Saarlandes, alongside my Master's in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

I specialise in memory- and compute-efficient deep learning pipelines, Docker-based deployment, and configurable training frameworks that other research projects can reuse. With a grounding in statistics and applied mathematics, I'm comfortable owning a model from the data pipeline all the way to a deployed, evaluated system.

Off the clock: code maverick, tech & music addict — and usually dancing, travelling, reading, cooking or sketching. The “Astro” in AstroK5 is earned: I've contributed asteroid observations to the International Astronomical Search Collaboration.
